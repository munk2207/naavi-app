# B12k — Closure Record

**Work item:** [[B12k]] — Naavi is too slow to answer on voice.
**Opened:** 2026-08-28, on Wael's report that prospects had complained.
**Closed:** 2026-08-30.
**Outcome:** **INVESTIGATED AND PARTIALLY REMEDIED. The item's own 5-second bar was NOT met.**

**Read this before reopening the subject.** The remaining lever is identified, measured, and tracked as [[B12m]]. Re-running B12k's investigation would rediscover what is written here.

---

## 1. What the item was, in the caller's terms

Naavi takes too long to answer on a phone call. A simple lookup is fine. Anything needing the caller's calendar and saved notes ran eight to twenty seconds, and twice in one session ran to nearly two minutes — by which point the caller had hung up and the answer reached nobody.

**The bar Wael fixed at Phase 0, before any measurement existed:**

> **Under 5 seconds for an ordinary turn. Never more than 30 seconds.**

---

## 2. What was measured — the part that has lasting value

**A 30-turn baseline on staging, precommitted as a practical trial set and explicitly not a statistical threshold.**

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| **Median turn** | **8 736 ms** |
| Turns over the 5 s bar | **24 of 30** |
| Turns over the 30 s ceiling | **1 of 30** |
| Fastest turn observed | 4 643 ms |

**Model comparison, controlled, both arms forced explicitly** — required because the classifier's own routing proved unstable:

| Model | Median |
|---|---|
| `claude-sonnet-4-6` | 4 252 ms |
| `claude-haiku-4-5` | 1 977 ms |

**Four serial context calls: ≈260 ms total**, against a 750 ms gate fixed before any data existed.

## 3. What shipped, and what it achieved

**All on the voice `staging` branch, `e2dcb0f` … `fb6546c`. Production `main` is untouched at `5dff3d5`.**

| Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **1 — instrumentation** | **Shipped.** Timing on every context call, an event-loop lag probe, and per-turn markers. Before this, four serial calls were uninstrumented and lag was unmeasured |
| **2 — staging model switch** | **Shipped, then cleared.** `B12K_FORCE_MODEL` existed to run the controlled comparison. Removed; verified absent from the staging config |
| **3a — model selection** | **Shipped, narrowly.** Five check-in phrases moved to the trivial fast path: **6.76 s → 3.05 s** through the endpoint, **2 411 ms on a real phone** |
| **3b — context path** | **RULED OUT by measurement.** ≈260 ms against a 750 ms gate. Nothing built |
| **3c — bounded calls** | **Shipped.** Three outbound calls bounded at 10 s. Four production turns had previously run 104, 110, 122 and 140 seconds with no timeout anywhere on the path |
| **3d — answer length** | **CLOSED by Wael.** The premise was wrong — see §5 |

**Nine permanent regression tests** across `b12k-fast-path.ts` and `b12k-bounded-calls.ts`, registered in `tests/runner.ts`, plus four voice-PIN tests repaired along the way. Both suites lift the live regex and the live helper out of the voice server rather than restating them, so neither can pass against a drifted copy.

---

## 4. ⭐ Why the bar was not met, stated plainly

**Naavi's median turn is unchanged. Five check-in phrases are faster; nothing else is.**

**The model lever is exhausted, and that is a finding rather than a failure.** Haiku beat Sonnet on all four question types tested. It was then vetoed on quality, measured rather than impressioned:

- **It stated the WORK address as the HOME address — 3 trials out of 3.**
- **It truncated open questions at the token ceiling — 3 trials out of 3.**

**So only check-ins moved**, and a permanent test now guards that boundary: two adversarial negatives (*"can you hear me read my emails"*, *"Are you there when I call Bob?"*) fail the suite if the fast path ever widens into information requests.

**The context lever does not exist.** 3b assumed the four serial calls were costing meaningful time. They cost ≈260 ms.

**What that leaves is the real cause, and it is architectural:** Naavi waits until the whole answer exists before speaking any of it. On a hard question the caller hears silence for the entire generation. **That is [[B12m]]**, opened 2026-08-29 on Wael's approval under Rule 1b, and it is where the remaining time is.

---

## 5. ⭐ Two rulings by Wael that closed branches — do not reopen them

**3d — answer length. CLOSED.** The proposal was to cap how much Naavi says. His ruling:

> *"The issue that we are trying to address is the delay between last character Robert says and the first character Naavi answer. The length of Naavi answer is part of the business process — the waiting until she speaks, not how long she speaks."*

And, decisively, on the suggestion that she think less on hard questions:

> *"That is the price you pay when you ask a difficult question… you can NOT tell me stop thinking, I do not have time."*

**The honest-failure apparatus for 3c. REMOVED.** Five commits built machinery to change what Naavi says when a bound fires, on the assumption that *"I don't have that information in your records"* falsely implies the caller's records are empty. His ruling:

> *"If Naavi said I do not have the information, [it] does not mean that Robert does not have the information or the information does not exist. It simply means Naavi does not have the resources to answer. Nothing more."*

**And the measurement that settled it: the same question with the lookup succeeding returns that sentence word for word.** There was never a distinct failure case. **The apparatus solved a problem that did not exist and produced two defects on the way** — a grammar bug, and a gate broad enough to replace a weather answer with *"I couldn't reach your saved notes."* All of it was removed; only the bound remains.

## 6. Governance record

**Full Phase 0–8, twice** — Stages 1–2 closed at their own Phase 8 on 2026-08-29, Stage 3 on 2026-08-30. ChatGPT reviewed every phase, with Wael relaying and issuing the gate decisions.

**The review was adversarial and changed the engineering, not only the prose.** Sixteen documents were returned. The corrections worth carrying forward:

- **A precommitted bar cannot be reinterpreted after the result is known** to mean "the part my change touched." Phase 7 declared itself *"complete"* after its own body had found the bar met for Stage 3 and not met for the call as a whole.
- **A declared gap has to be retired when it closes.** Phase 8 stated the reviewer had not seen new evidence — true when written, false when read, since their return *was* them engaging with it.
- **Gate C3** had made a rare event's recurrence a precondition for building protection against rare events.
- **A 90-turn statistical threshold** was manufactured from a single event in 30 trials.
- **A claim that Node freezes `process.env` at process start** was false; the real finding is Railway's — **set replaces the container, delete does not.**

**Wael's own challenges are recorded separately from the reviewer's**, because their form differs and the distinction matters:

- *"Give me your reference that I said that??"* — which exposed a narrow place-cache rule being cited as a general anti-cache position of his, and produced the `feedback_citation_is_not_a_warrant` memory.
- *"I must be sure — one test does not justify creating a new item."* — declining to open an item on a single observation.

## 7. What a future session should know

1. **Do not re-measure the context path.** ≈260 ms. It is not where the time goes.
2. **Do not widen the trivial fast path** without re-running the quality comparison. Haiku gets the home address wrong. A test will stop you, and the test is the point.
3. **The `commit=` marker in voice logs is a hardcoded April literal.** It proves nothing about what is deployed. Prove a voice deploy behaviourally — Architecture Reference §0d.
4. **`/test/ask` is not a phone.** It drives `askClaude` directly, with no Twilio, no Deepgram, no audio.
5. **Deleting a Railway variable does not restart the container.** Clear it *and* redeploy, then verify by behaviour.
6. **The remaining latency work is [[B12m]]**, not a reopening of this item.

---

## 8. Closure

**B12k closes as investigated, with its bar not met and that stated rather than dressed up.**

**What it delivered:** the ability to measure at all, one branch ruled out by measurement, two branches closed by the Product Owner's reading of the product, five phrases materially faster, a two-minute failure mode bounded at ten seconds, and nine permanent tests holding all of it in place.

**What it did not deliver: a faster Naavi.** The complaint Wael reported from prospects stands, and the lever that would answer it is [[B12m]].

**No production promotion was made, proposed, or authorized by this work item.**
